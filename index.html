Questions

Is the first class really free?

Yes. Your 1st class is free after you sign up. All other classes are paid, between Rs. 1,200 and Rs. 2,500 (prices simulated, no real payment is taken).

I have never exercised. Is that okay?

Yes. Choose a Beginner or All Levels class. Please consult a professional before starting a new routine.

Is this a real booking?

No. FitZone is a fictional studio and the pass is a sample only.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, a towel, a water bottle and clean sports shoes.

Can I change my class later?

Yes. In a real studio you could reschedule up to 4 hours before class. This is a demo, so nothing is actually booked.

Do you offer diet advice?

We share general healthy-eating ideas on the Diet page. For personal advice, please see a dietitian.
